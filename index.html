Process

How we co-create

Co-creation is not just an idea — it's how I work.

1

Learn

We start with a conversation where you teach me your priorities, communication style, and pain points. I don't assume. I learn.

2

Build

I build a first version of a system — calendar, email, travel template, dashboard. You use it for a few days and tell me what works.

3

Refine

Based on your feedback, I adjust. Sometimes we jump on a quick call; other times I send a video walkthrough. The system becomes ours.

4

Document

Once the system works, I document it clearly. You can run it without me if needed — but you keep me because I make it better over time.

5

Review

Every Monday for 20 minutes, we review what's working, what's not, and what to automate next.

Partnership

How we can work together

Onboarding, communication rhythms, and reporting — designed for clarity.

Onboarding: The First 5 Days

Day 1 Limited, revocable access to key tools. Dedicated encrypted device + 2FA.
Days 2–3 60-min call to review priorities, style, time zones, VIPs.
Day 4 Quiet inbox & calendar management — then a handled summary for feedback.
Day 5 Adjust processes. I take over fully with a weekly check-in.

Communication Rhythms

  • Daily: Morning brief with top 3 priorities, meeting agendas, urgent requests.
  • Weekly: 20-minute Monday sync to review the week ahead.
  • Urgent: Flagged immediately via your preferred channel (call / text / Slack).
  • I never make commitments on your behalf without confirmation.
  • I adapt to your hours, not mine.

Reporting Style

  • Inbox & Calendar Reports: End-of-day summary of emails handled, meetings booked, follow-ups.
  • Project Updates: Weekly status dashboards with progress, blockers, next steps.
  • Travel & Expense Summaries: Post-trip one-pagers with receipts and breakdowns.
  • All reports are visual, concise, and designed to be read under 5 minutes.

Core Hours & Boundaries

  • My core hours align to your standard workday.
  • Outside hours: I check Slack once, roughly two hours after your end-of-day, for urgent matters only.
  • Time zone guarantee: I never ask you to take meetings outside your defined working hours.
Rhythm

A day in the life

How I show up, every day, to keep you moving forward.

First 30–60 minutes
  • Scan and triage email using Inbox Zero, flagging urgent and drafting replies
  • Send a daily morning brief with top 3 priorities, meeting agendas and urgent requests
  • Review calendar for conflicts, back-to-back meetings, or missing prep time
  • Proactively block focus time or reschedule non-urgent items
Mid-morning
  • Prepare meeting materials (agenda, documents, attendee summaries)
  • Update project dashboards and nudge team/vendors on pending tasks
  • Handle urgent requests (travel changes, scheduling conflicts) without interrupting you
Early afternoon
  • Process incoming emails and schedule new meeting requests
  • Confirm appointments for the next day
Last hour of your workday
  • Send end-of-day summary: emails handled, follow-ups, tasks completed, tomorrow's preview
  • Prepare for next day — reschedule low-priority items, draft tomorrow's morning brief
Weekly / As needed
  • 20-minute Monday sync
  • Friday recap on wins, blockers, and preparation for the next week
Monthly system review
  • Review email filters and rules — remove outdated, add new senders, adjust labels
  • Audit automations — confirm they're working correctly
  • Review project management dashboard — archive, rename, add priority categories
  • Document system changes in our shared handover note
